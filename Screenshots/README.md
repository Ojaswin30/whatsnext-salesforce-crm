| **`03_dealer_management.png`** | Vehicle Dealers Tab | Dealership partner accounts showing dealer codes, locations, and contact info. |

| **`05_order_management.png`** | Vehicle Orders Tab | Order records showing customer lookups, ordered vehicles, order dates, and status lifecycle badges. |
